01 / about

Build things that can be checked.

I am a first-year Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China. My focus is AI infrastructure and high-performance computing. I am working through CUDA in a public practice repository, and I keep contest-style algorithm practice running beside the degree.

I do not yet have published work. I would rather be judged on small, checkable artifacts: kernels that run, scripts with logs, and notes that record what I got wrong.
